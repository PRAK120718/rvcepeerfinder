Export Your Data

Request a copy of all your data stored in Neopeer. We'll prepare your data package and send it to your registered email.

Your data package will include:

  • Profile information and student code
  • All aspiration profiles
  • Onboarding Interview history and transcripts
  • 'Get to Know You' recordings (if available)
  • Match recommendations and interactions
  • Scheduled meetings and history
  • Meeting notes and summaries
  • Privacy and consent preferences

We'll send the download link to this email address

Processing Time: Your data package will be prepared within 24-48 hours. You'll receive an email with a secure download link that expires in 7 days.

Note: Large data packages may take longer to prepare. If you don't receive the email within 48 hours, please contact support.
