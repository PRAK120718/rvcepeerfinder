Data Privacy & Consent

Aligned with India's DPDP Act, 2023

📌 1. Introduction
NeoPeer (“we”, “our”, “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and Rules, 2025.
📌 2. Types of Data We Collect
A. Identity & Contact: Name, email, phone number, profile photo.
B. Education & Aspiration: Current course, institution, goals, prep details.
C. Behavioral & Preference: Onboarding responses, usage patterns.
D. Contextual: Challenges faced, feedback scores.
We adhere to Data Minimisation: we collect only data necessary for the stated purposes of matching mentors and peers.
📌 3. Purpose of Collection
Your data is processed for: User onboarding, peer/mentor matching, providing notifications, improving product quality, and legal compliance.
📌 4. Withdrawal of Consent
You may withdraw your consent at any time via account settings or by emailing our Data Protection Officer. Processing of your data will stop immediately upon valid withdrawal.
📌 5. Account Deletion (Right to Erasure)
Under the DPDP Act, you have the Right to Erasure. You can request the deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time through the "Delete Account" option in settings.
📌 6. Grievance Redressal
If you have any grievances regarding the processing of your personal data, you may contact our Grievance Officer at:
Email: grievance@neopeer.app
We will acknowledge your grievance within 24 hours and resolve it within the statutory period.
📌 7. Verifiable Consent
If you are a minor (under 18), we require verifiable consent from a parent or lawful guardian before processing your data.
