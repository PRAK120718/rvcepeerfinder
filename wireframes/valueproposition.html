Breaking the Ceiling of Exploration

Helping aspirants move from the exploration phase to the execution phase, enabling them to appear stable, confident and mature in their daily conversations.

The 4 Phases of Any Aspiration

1
Discovery: Learning where they are and what they need to do.

Requires only a mentor from a similar background.

2
Planning: Creating a plan of action.

Majority will want both, peers and mentors to remove ambiguity.

3
Execution: Re-evaluating the plan with on-going trends.
4
Attempt: Applying emotional and raw intelligence with best chances of success.
The app provides options to connect with both a peer and a mentor to optimize this combination.

Research Insights & System Strategy

Identity-Free Guidance

Aspiration is personal. Revealing identity can create groupism or dis-engagement. Explaining why guidance helps is more valuable than revealing who gave it.

The app never reveals identity, promoting real, open, and judgement-free communication.
Sustainable Mentorship

Mentors need incentives to forgo other work repeatedly. While students value mentorship, they are hesitant to pay for "peers" as it's a new concept.

Capped pricing (₹49 - ₹199). Commitment fee for peers is minimal and promises a full refund if no fit is found in 5 recommendations.
Safety & Accountability

Mischief can happen at any time. The system must be able to detect problems while maintaining a "pure software" feel.

Meetings are transcribed for quality purposes, allowing for audit and detection of misuse without invasive recording.
Software Precision vs. AI Cost

Voice AI costs are significantly higher than what students can pay. Sustainability requires a high-volume, software-first approach.

Very strong decision-tree algorithm built on behavioral psychology to provide 80% of the value at a sustainable price point.
The Universal Role

A mentor is anyone who has achieved; an aspirant is anyone who wants to achieve. Background is the variable that shapes "right" vs "wrong" advice.

Verification via institutional ID. The same person can join as both a mentor and an aspirant.
Breaking Meeting Inertia

Students often don't know what to talk about during the first meeting, leading to interest loss.

The app provides a list of aspiration-tailored ice-breaker questions to trigger meaningful value immediately.
Institutional Partnership

Marketing is the biggest cost. Trust is the second. Neopeer "gets the job done" where others fail because it builds on existing campus trust.

Expecting college administration to support marketing in exchange for real, sustainable value creation for the student body.
Ecosystem Growth

Students in tier-2 colleges often have higher aspirant ratios and want to learn from those who have achieved.

Platform logic extends to RVU students to start with, building a cross-pollination of background-relevant mentorship.
"Nobody is a pioneer, everyone is a neopeer."

Creating a 'Pay It Forward' culture where giving help builds your support system for the long term. Accelerate others today; be accelerated tomorrow.
