Delete My Platform Data

This will permanently delete your profile details, conversation history, and matching insights.

⚠️ Warning
This action cannot be undone. All conversation history, preferences, and personality insights collected from your voice conversations will be permanently deleted.

What will be deleted:

  • All voice conversation recordings
  • Conversation transcripts
  • Personality insights and analysis
  • Aspiration profiles created through conversations
  • Agent memory and context

What will NOT be deleted:

  • Your account and student code
  • Scheduled meetings
  • Matched connections
  • Meeting summaries and notes
  • Your privacy and notification settings

💡 Tip: You can start a new conversation with your agent anytime after deletion. The agent will begin fresh without previous context.
