Delete My Account

This action will permanently delete your Neopeer account

⚠️ Permanent Action
Deleting your account cannot be undone. All your data will be permanently removed.

What will be deleted:

  • Your account and student code
  • All aspiration profiles
  • All agent conversation history
  • Voice recordings and transcripts
  • Match recommendations
  • Scheduled and past meetings
  • Meeting notes and summaries
  • All preferences and settings

What happens to your connections:

  • Connected peers will be notified that your account has been deleted
  • Ongoing conversations will be ended
  • Scheduled meetings will be cancelled

Alternative options: Instead of deleting, you could pause your account or delete just your agent memory. Consider these options in Settings.
