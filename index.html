🛡️ Institutional Framework & Ethical Guidelines

Establishing trust through rigorous data ethics, risk mitigation, and institutional alignment.

🚫 Usage Guidelines

Strict protocols to maintain student trust and institutional integrity.

  • • No Academic Targeting: Individual data is never used for grading or student profiling. Data remains anonymous at the institutional level.
  • • Strict Privacy: Student data is never shared or commercialized with third-party recruiters or EdTech companies.
  • • Safe Disclosure: Information shared during discovery is never used as grounds for disciplinary action.
  • • Purpose-Limited AI: Voice and text analysis are used solely for sentiment and matching, not for permanent biometric surveillance.

💡 Institutional Value

How Neopeer strengthens the campus ecosystem.

Success Continuity Loop

Capturing the 'silent struggles' of seniors and matching them with juniors ensures tribal success knowledge stays within the campus.

Faculty Support

Reduces the load on faculty mentors by providing students with relatable peer support for everyday hurdles.

Campus Pulse Insights

Aggregate, de-identified trends reveal high-stress areas (subjects/semesters), allowing proactive improvements to student services.

⚠️ Risk Mitigation & Platform Integrity

Potential Concern Mitigation Strategy
"Spyware" Perception Anonymity-by-Design: Discovery is 100% anonymous. The institution only sees de-identified, aggregated sentiment reports to improve campus life.
Institutional Liability Verification & Accountability: Every mentor is campus-verified. Systems include built-in reporting and session-end disclaimers labeling advice as peer-to-peer.
Commercial Exploitation Capped Honorarium: Relevancy-based matching prioritizes support over tutoring. Mentorship fees are capped to maintain a community-first culture.
Echo Chambers Growth-Match Logic: AI algorithm suggests "bridge" mentors—those who achieved similar goals from different initial backgrounds—to ensure diverse perspectives.
